Privacy Policy for FairSplit

Last updated: August 2026

FairSplit ("we", "our", "the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information We Collect

FairSplit does not require an account and does not collect or store any personal information on external servers. All bill amounts, tip calculations, and split details you enter stay on your device during your session and are not transmitted anywhere.

FairSplit displays ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to show relevant ads, in accordance with Google's own privacy policy. You can review Google's practices at https://policies.google.com/privacy.
